Hygiene Protocol

Sanitation Process

1

Clean & Wipe Down

All surfaces, lamps, and armrests are wiped with hospital-grade disinfectant before each appointment.

2

Sterilize Metal Tools

Cuticle pushers, nippers, and other metal tools are autoclave-sterilized between every client.

3

Fresh Disposables

Nail files, buffers, and cotton pads are single-use and discarded after every appointment.

4

Hand Hygiene

Hands are washed and sanitized before touching any client's nails.
